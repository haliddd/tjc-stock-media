# Joanna Mini Beta Upload, Naming, And Tagging Guide

## Upload

Upload photos only. Use a focused batch, not a whole archive dump.

Good test uploads:

- Bible study photos.
- Prayer or chapel/group worship from safe angles.
- Church property or ministry context.
- Design-friendly backgrounds with negative space.
- Small curated nature photos useful for church design.

Do not upload:

- Video or audio.
- Unknown copyrighted media.
- Sensitive people/minors media without context.
- Random large albums.
- Source files needing deletion, renaming, or cleanup.

## Minimal Metadata

Fill:

- Title/name.
- Event/source.
- Contributor.
- Rights/source type.
- Usage intent.
- People/minors visible.
- Notes.

Optional but useful:

- Date.
- Ministry/team.
- Suggested tags.
- Proof/license link.
- Sensitivity notes.

## Naming Pattern

Use readable names when you create new files:

`YYYY-event-short-description-contributor`

Example:

`2024-bible-study-table-hali`

Filename helps humans, but metadata and approved tags matter more than filename.

## Tags

Use simple tags Joanna can judge:

- Bible
- baptism
- prayer
- fellowship
- worship
- chapel
- youth
- church property
- flowers
- negative space

Reviewers approve final tags. Rights/copyright uncertainty always defaults to review. Found/pretty does not mean approved/public.

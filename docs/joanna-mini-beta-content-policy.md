# Joanna Mini Beta Content Policy

Purpose: guide Joanna content-manager feedback for photo-only mini beta.

## Prioritize

- Baptism in living water.
- Bible study.
- Prayer.
- Chapel or group worship from safe, respectful angles.
- Church property and ministry context.
- TJC ministry activity that explains real church life.
- Design-friendly negative space for slides, web, newsletters, and print.

## Use Carefully

- Generic nature photos are allowed only as curated useful content.
- Nature should serve design needs: calm backgrounds, negative space, seasonal context, or church-adjacent visuals.
- Do not bulk-import unlimited pretty photos.

## Rights Buckets

- Own photos: still need people/minors/sensitive-context review before public use.
- Church-owned photos: faster review, but not automatic public approval.
- Canva/Pexels/Unsplash-style external assets: require license/source note and attribution check.
- Uncertain assets: stay `Needs Review / Do Not Publish`.

## Always Keep In Review Until Cleared

- Children or youth.
- Sacrament context.
- Worship, sermon, prayer, music, hymn, choir, or livestream-related photos.
- Unknown people.
- Unclear contributor media.
- Copyright or license uncertainty.

## Decision Rule

A found, pretty, or useful photo is not approved/public. Approval requires reviewer, date, usage scope, and notes.
